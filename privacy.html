English

Effective date: 21.11.2026

QuietRoll helps you sort the photos and videos on your iPhone. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • QuietRoll has no account, no servers and no analytics.
  • Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied or shared.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party code in the app.
  • The app sends no data to the developer. The only exceptions are an email you choose to send us, and crash reports that Apple shares if you allow it.

Your photos and videos

QuietRoll asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos and move the ones you choose to Recently Deleted. Everything happens on your iPhone.

  • QuietRoll reads photos and videos only to display them. It does not copy them or store them anywhere else.
  • Deleting always goes through the iOS confirmation dialog. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for a limited time, as with any deletion in Photos.
  • To find similar photos, QuietRoll compares small versions of photos taken close together. The results exist only in memory while you are sorting and are never saved.
  • While you sort, QuietRoll shows only what is already on your iPhone and downloads nothing from iCloud. If you use iCloud Photos, a photo is downloaded from iCloud only when you open it in the Trash to see it in full size, and a video only when you choose to play it. The download comes from your own iCloud through Apple, not through us.
  • You can change or remove photo access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

What QuietRoll stores on your iPhone

QuietRoll keeps a small amount of information on your device so it can remember your progress. None of it leaves your iPhone, and none of it is sent to the developer.

Purchases

QuietRoll Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. QuietRoll checks with Apple on your device whether Pro is active and does not keep its own copy.

App Store ratings

QuietRoll may ask Apple to show the standard rating request after a successful clean-up. Apple decides whether it appears. QuietRoll never sees your rating or review.

Contacting support

If you tap Contact Us, QuietRoll opens a draft in your own email app. The draft already contains three lines to help us understand a problem: the app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model (for example "iPhone17,5"). It does not contain your device name, your photos or anything from your library. You can read and change the draft before sending it. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

If you write to us, we receive what you send: your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we delete your emails 12 months after our last exchange. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple shares anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with developers. QuietRoll does not collect or send anything itself for this. You can turn it off there at any time.

What QuietRoll does not collect

Photo content, location, faces or people, album names, contacts, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash reports from third-party services, or any account information. QuietRoll does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

QuietRoll does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app sends no data to the developer, we hold nothing about you unless you have emailed us. You can:

  • remove what QuietRoll stores on your iPhone by resetting the review history or deleting the app (the free-deletion counter described above is the exception);
  • ask for a copy of your support emails, or ask us to delete them, by writing to the address below.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK may give you further rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. Write to us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the way QuietRoll handles data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the new version of the app is released.

  • Du kan til enhver tid ændre eller fjerne adgangen under Indstillinger › Anonymitet & sikkerhed › Fotos.
